Preface

This book is being written to provide it as notes for students in my undergraduate marketing research class. It will be updated as more content is created and more feedback is received. It presumes that the reader is familiar with terms used in basic statistics and does not have a phobia of numbers. Please approach the content with an open mind.

About me

I am a Professor of Marketing in the School of Business Administration at Gonzaga University, where I teach marketing research, data visualization and business analytics.
